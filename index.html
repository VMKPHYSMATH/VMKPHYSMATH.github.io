Research Interests

Non-Hermitian Quantum Impurity Physics

PT symmetry, exceptional points, and emergent phenomena in driven impurity systems with complex spectra.

Kondo & Strongly Correlated Systems

Slave-boson mean-field theory, thermodynamic Bethe ansatz, and non-Fermi liquid fixed points.

Mathematical Physics & RG

Biorthogonal quantum mechanics, Yang-Baxter integrability, Puiseux expansions, and RG flows.

Topological & Edge-State Physics

Anderson impurities in topological edge states, cavity-mediated interactions, and symmetry-protected phases.

Non-Equilibrium & Open Quantum Systems

Keldysh field theory, CTQMC, Lindbladian dynamics, and driven quantum dots.

Dynamical Mean Field Theory

Non-equilibrium DMFT, soft-gap Anderson models, and quantum criticality in lattice Hamiltonians.
