Four complementary research directions, each with a physical question, mathematical approach and verifiable output.

01 / Many-body theory · Preprint

Many-body exceptional points at finite interaction

Question: Can an interacting quantum impurity retain exact solvability while its eigenstates merge at an exceptional point?

Approach: Equal-velocity Anderson impurity, dressed rapidities, Yang–Baxter integrability and a non-diagonalizable boundary twist.

Result: In the specified integrable model, each spin-S multiplet forms a Jordan block of order 2S + 1 at the exceptional point. The construction also connects Bethe-root contraction to Laguerre polynomials and a large-multiplet zero distribution.

Scope: curvature, unequal velocities and nonscalar channel couplings are outside the theorem.

02 / Open quantum systems · Preprint

Emergent non-Hermiticity from Hermitian driving

Question: How can exceptional points appear in a microscopic model with Hermitian dynamics?

Approach: Eliminate a driven spin-orbit-coupled bath using causal retarded Green functions; analyze complex resonance poles and impurity response.

Result: A passive retarded impurity kernel supports a nonlinear resonance EP, verified by discriminant winding, pole exchange and half-winding. The revised work also studies SOC continuation and interacting charge-fluctuating continuum saddle solutions.

Scope: thermodynamic interacting exceptional-point survival is an open question, not an established result.

03 / Quantum materials · Preprint with Sharona Horta

Quantum geometry seen by electron spectroscopy

Question: Can bulk low-loss electron energy-loss spectroscopy reveal the geometry of electronic wavefunctions?

Approach: 32-band DFT–Wannier Hamiltonian, full position-corrected Wannier-gauge matrix elements, occupied-band projectors and a restricted optical spectral sum rule.

Result: The revised position-corrected restricted optical weight is nearly isotropic despite strongly anisotropic band masses: central armchair/zigzag ratio 0.978, with tested numerical sensitivity 0.973–0.986. The optical weight is recovered from the reconstructed absorptive dielectric response, not directly from the raw EELS loss moment.

Potential application: connecting first-principles quantum geometry to experimentally accessible spectral moments.

04 / Strong correlations · Peer-reviewed

Kondo physics, criticality and dissipative edge states

Question: How do spin-orbit coupling, bath spectral structure and dissipation alter the screening of quantum impurities?

Approach: Renormalization group, slave-boson theory, Anderson impurity models and collaborative dynamical mean-field theory.

Outputs: Peer-reviewed studies on PT-symmetric Rashba Kondo physics (PRB 2022), emergent soft-gap models (PRB 2023) and nonlinear/dissipative edge-state impurities (SciPost 2025).

Research Interests

Non-Hermitian Quantum Impurity Physics

PT symmetry, exceptional points, and emergent phenomena in driven impurity systems with complex spectra.

Kondo & Strongly Correlated Systems

Slave-boson mean-field theory, thermodynamic Bethe ansatz, and non-Fermi liquid fixed points.

Mathematical Physics & RG

Biorthogonal quantum mechanics, Yang-Baxter integrability, Puiseux expansions, and RG flows.

Topological & Edge-State Physics

Anderson impurities in topological edge states, cavity-mediated interactions, and symmetry-protected phases.

Non-Equilibrium & Open Quantum Systems

Keldysh field theory, CTQMC, Lindbladian dynamics, and driven quantum dots.

Dynamical Mean Field Theory

Non-equilibrium DMFT, soft-gap Anderson models, and quantum criticality in lattice Hamiltonians.

Quantum Geometry of Materials

Wannier quantum geometry, quantum metric, direction-resolved quantum weight, and low-loss electron energy-loss spectroscopy in anisotropic materials.
