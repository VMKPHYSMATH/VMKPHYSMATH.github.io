Research profile

Theoretical condensed matter physicist developing analytical and computational methods for strongly correlated, nonequilibrium and open quantum systems. Research combines exact integrability, renormalization group, Green functions, electronic structure and quantum geometry with reproducible Python and Julia workflows.

Selected contributions

  • Finite-U Anderson integrability (2026): Yang–Baxter and twisted RTT construction, unipotent boundary twist, spin-multiplet Jordan structure and Bethe-root contraction in the specified integrable model.
  • Driven Dirac impurity (2025–26): passive retarded kernel emerging from Hermitian driving; causal resonance-pole exceptional points in the noninteracting benchmark and finite-interaction diagnostics.
  • Quantum geometry and EELS (2026): 32-band DFT–Wannier response, analytic momentum derivatives, gauge-invariant projectors and restricted spectral sum rules in black phosphorus.
  • Correlations and quantum criticality (2022–25): peer-reviewed work on non-Hermitian Rashba–Kondo physics, DMFT soft-gap criticality and dissipative edge-state impurities.
